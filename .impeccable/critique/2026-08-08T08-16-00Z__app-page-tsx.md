## Design Specificity Verdict

**Partially authored, drifting category-generic.** The copy is distinctly owner-voiced ("A nine-room guest house in Mmabatho, Mafikeng… no booking platform in between") and the terracotta/walnut/cream/gold palette + Playfair/Inter pairing feel warm and boutique. But the visual layer is interchangeable: coloured `PhotoPlaceholder` divs stand in for the property, the three-journey structure is the standard hotel-site pattern, and nothing communicates Mafikeng or family-run beyond the words. Any 9-room guest house anywhere could ship this unchanged.

**Deterministic scan:** impeccable detector on the public source files → `[]` (exit 0, clean). URL scans unavailable in this environment (detector needs `puppeteer` for URL mode); the clean result therefore reflects static/regex engines only. Browser evidence: 0 console errors across all 5 routes × 2 viewports (one hydration warning on /book during first compile did not reproduce on warm reload — artifact), 0 horizontal overflow, 0 broken/empty images, 0 nav-overlap, all screenshots at /tmp/critique-shots/.

## Overall Impression

A warm, well-unified, honestly-written site that is one real photo and one accessible calendar away from feeling finished. The single biggest opportunity: replace PhotoPlaceholder with the actual lodge — it out-converts every other change combined.

## What's Working

1. **Voice discipline** — plain, factual, owner-voiced copy is a genuine differentiator; the site says "family-run" and feels it in the words.
2. **Brand consistency** — palette, typography, pills, motion, focus treatment uniform across all surfaces including admin (post-polish).
3. **Recap sidebar** — the sticky "Your stay" panel with live indicative total is excellent recognition-though-recall design; the "Prefer a different room?" cross-sell is a smart conversion touch.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Custom calendar + room cards are keyboard-inaccessible.** Every date cell and room card is a `<div onClick>` in `BookingForm.tsx` — no `role`, `tabindex`, or key handler. *Why:* a keyboard or screen-reader user (Sam) cannot select dates or choose a room at all — the core booking action fails for them. *Fix:* render days as real `<button>`s (disabled for past dates, `aria-pressed` for selection) and room cards as focusable buttons; reuse the existing `:focus-visible` ring. *Command:* `$impeccable adapt` (or `audit` first).
- **[P1] No real photography.** Every `PhotoPlaceholder` on a hospitality site is lost trust and lost conversion. *Fix:* ship real lodge photos (client must provide) — hero, rooms, events. *Command:* asset work outside the skill's commands; surface via `$impeccable bolder` after assets land.
- **[P1] Dead contact/confirmation channels.** WhatsApp links are `#` placeholders and the WhatsApp notifier is stubbed (`console.log`) — the core promise "confirm by WhatsApp within minutes" cannot happen in production. *Why:* the day a real booking lands, staff see only a log line. *Fix:* real number + real notifier before launch. *Command:* backend work, but `$impeccable clarify` keeps the UI honest meanwhile.
- **[P2] Meal-price inconsistency.** Wizard hardcodes R175/R300; corporate page quotes R150–200/R250–350. *Why:* users comparing pages lose trust. *Fix:* single source of truth for meal pricing. *Command:* `$impeccable clarify`.
- **[P2] POP "upload" is filename-only.** The file-drop records a filename and never calls `/api/upload`. The drop zone is also a `<div>` with no keyboard access. *Why:* users discover it silently does nothing. *Fix:* wire the upload or relabel as "POP reference only". *Command:* `$impeccable clarify`.
- **[P2] Step indicator is decorative.** Dots 3–4 never reflect state (meals/details), and the client doesn't validate dates before submit — the user only learns dates were missing after submitting. *Fix:* drive dots from real state; pre-validate dates on submit with inline field errors. *Command:* `$impeccable polish`.
- **[P3] Focus treatment unverified at scale.** 25–44 elements per route report `outline:none` at rest — expected under `:focus-visible`, but the branded ring needs a real keyboard tab-through, especially over the calendar once it becomes focusable. *Command:* `$impeccable audit`.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Sam (accessibility):** cannot book. The calendar days and room cards are `<div onClick>` — no keyboard path to the primary action; past dates look disabled (grey, cursor-not-allowed) but are still announced. Contrast is committed AA and rings exist, but this one structural gap breaks the whole journey.
- **Jordan (first-timer):** hero CTA is clear, but the "call us / WhatsApp" affordances are dead `#` links, there's no FAQ, and "Iphe Lerato" is unexplained flavor. First questions go unanswered.
- **Casey (mobile):** 44px targets committed and primary CTAs well-placed, but the recap sidebar stacks below the whole form on mobile, wizard progress is lost on interruption (no draft), and the POP upload is friction on a phone.
- **Riley (stress tester):** the triple of *placeholder phone + stubbed WhatsApp + filename-only POP upload* means "appears to work but silently does nothing" — the exact failure class Riley hunts.

## Minor Observations

- "Iphe Lerato" appears without a translation/gloss in the footer.
- Corporate page's 24h quote promise has no "how you'll receive it" note at the CTA.
- The detector's URL mode needs `puppeteer`; static scan is clean but that's a partial signal.
- Temp evidence scripts (`scripts/_critique-b.mjs`) should be cleaned up.

## Questions to Consider

- What if the hero showed the actual lodge — would that one change out-convert every other improvement combined?
- The site promises "WhatsApp within minutes" — what happens the day a real booking lands with the notifier still stubbed?
- Do the three journeys earn their complexity, or would one booking path with visible pricing convert more of the 50-guest event crowd?
- Would a live availability snapshot on the homepage move more people than the wizard's calendar?
